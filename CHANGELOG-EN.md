The trainings improvements update.

## Features

- added optional exercise notes up to 100 characters, displayed in the exercise list and during workouts,
- completing a workout now shows a one-time summary of session duration, completed sets, and reps,
- added weekly streaks: a calendar week with at least three completed workouts is highlighted in orange,
- the home calendar supports switching months without opening another screen and displays the monthly workout count and current weekly streak below it,
- added a separate option to delete workout history together with workout and lifted-weight statistics.

## Fixes

- the calendar and statistics panel now use one data source and record a workout only after full completion,
- leaving an active workout now requires confirmation and does not save an unfinished session,
- an expanded exercise header and its details now form one card without an internal gap or rounded corners.
